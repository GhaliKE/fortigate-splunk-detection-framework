# Noise reduction

The wide, balanced, and strict profiles trade investigation volume against
retention. The balanced profile is the reference choice because it requires at
least 50 firewall decisions, 25 blocked connections, and an 80% blocking
ratio. Calibration retained 119, 74, and 46 sources respectively.

The 74-source calibration and 81-source live result are not contradictory:
they were measured at different times while data continued to arrive.
