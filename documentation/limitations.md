# Limitations

- No labeled ground truth is available.
- Accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 were not measured.
- Score weights are heuristic.
- Thresholds are environment-specific.
- The historical measurement period is limited.
- The statistical model is univariate.
- Business and asset context is limited.
- No automatic blocking or incident creation is performed.
- Human investigation remains required.

The absence of an anomaly or outlier is not evidence that no threat exists.
